Welcome to OkiJALT!

Since 1980, Okinawa JALT has served language educators with two core missions: professional development for language teachers and promoting language education in the community. Our events feature world-renowned scholars alongside local language professionals, creating a dynamic space for sharing ideas and improving teaching quality.

Part of the JALT Network

Okinawa JALT is a chapter of the Japan Association for Language Teaching, Asia's largest language education organization with ~3,000 members across 31 chapters and 24 special interest groups

🌏

Est. 1975

Founded in 1975 and recognized as an NPO in 1999, JALT is dedicated to improving language teaching and learning throughout Japan and internationally.

📍

31 Chapters

From Hokkaido to Okinawa, JALT chapters host regular meetings, workshops, and events bringing language educators together nationwide.

🔬

24 SIGs

Special Interest Groups focusing on specialized topics from CALL to Study Abroad, Gender Awareness to Mind, Brain & Education.

2000s — Building Community & Regular Programming

During this decade, Okinawa JALT established regular monthly meetings at Okinawa Christian Junior College (Nishihara) and Meio University (Nago). The chapter built strong traditions including:

  • 📚 Monthly presentations on teaching techniques and research
  • 🏖️ Annual summer beach BBQs and family picnics
  • 🎉 End-of-year Bonnenkai celebrations
  • ✍️ "Power of the Pen" writing contests for high school students
  • 👥 "My Share" sessions for local teachers to exchange ideas

🏆 Congratulations

Honoring a Lifetime of Leadership: David Kluge Receives JALT’s Highest Honor

The Okinawa Chapter of the Japan Association for Language Teaching (JALT) is thrilled to share a moment of incredible pride for our local ELT community.

Our very own David Kluge has been awarded the prestigious JALT Lifetime Achievement Award, the highest honor granted by the Japan Association for Language Teaching. This national recognition celebrates David’s extraordinary dedication, decades of selfless service, and transformative impact on language education and leadership across Japan and right here in Okinawa.

For many years, David has been a steadfast champion of language educators, serving as a vital mentor, leader, and advocate within our chapter and the broader national organization. His vision, passion, and tireless commitment to fostering teacher growth have enriched the professional lives of countless colleagues and students alike.

“David’s award is a testament not only to his monumental contributions over the years, but also to the spirit of warmth, generosity, and scholarship he brings to our chapter every single day.”

Please join all of us at Okinawa JALT in offering our heartfelt congratulations to David on this well-deserved milestone!

Expanded Leadership

Okinawa JALT is growing its executive team with three new officer positions to better serve our members and community

NEW POSITION
🌊

Vice President

Executive Leadership

The Vice President supports the President in all chapter operations and serves as Acting President when needed. This role provides vital leadership continuity and ensures our chapter runs smoothly across all functions.

Key Responsibilities

  • ✦ Acts on behalf of the President in their absence
  • ✦ Oversees inter-officer communication and coordination
  • ✦ Supports planning for chapter events and initiatives
  • ✦ Liaises with JALT national and other chapters
  • ✦ Assists with strategic planning and goal-setting
NEW POSITION
📖

Publications Chair

Research & Publications

The Publications Chair leads all chapter publishing activities, including the new Okinawa JALT Journal. This role champions member scholarship, manages editorial workflows, and helps Okinawa JALT contribute meaningfully to the wider EFL research community.

Key Responsibilities

  • ✦ Oversees the Okinawa JALT Journal from submission to publication
  • ✦ Recruits and coordinates peer reviewers and editorial board
  • ✦ Manages chapter newsletter and website content
  • ✦ Promotes member publication opportunities
  • ✦ Maintains archival records of chapter publications
NEW POSITION
🌱

Professional Development Advisor

Member Learning & Growth

A new role focused on member learning and growth, helping members develop as teachers, presenters, and scholars. Like the other new positions, it carries a one-year renewable term.

These positions are part of Okinawa JALT's ongoing commitment to building a sustainable, member-driven chapter. For more on our full leadership structure, see the Chapter Officers section or explore our Advisory & Leadership Boards.

Advisory & Leadership Framework

Four boards that connect experience, expertise, and community — strengthening Okinawa JALT from within and beyond

🏛️

Presidential Advisory Council

Strategic Guidance

A small council of senior members who advise the President on strategy, major decisions, and long-term direction. Provides continuity across leadership transitions and helps the chapter navigate complex challenges.

Membership: 3–5 senior/past presidents
Meetings: Quarterly or as needed
Role: Advisory (non-voting)
⚙️

Executive Officers Council

Operational Leadership

The core governing body of the chapter, comprising all elected and appointed officers. Coordinates chapter activities, allocates resources, and sets priorities for each program year — the engine of day-to-day chapter operations.

Okinawa JALT Journal

Share your scholarship with Okinawa and beyond: a supported, rigorous pathway to publication for researchers, practitioners, and students

Our Mission

The Okinawa JALT Journal is the peer-reviewed annual publication of the Okinawa Chapter of the Japan Association for Language Teaching (JALT). It publishes original research and reflective practice from language educators in Okinawa, across Japan, and beyond.

We believe every language educator, whether a seasoned researcher, a classroom practitioner, or someone just beginning their scholarly journey, has something worth sharing. The journal exists to give that work a home.

Journal at a Glance

  • 📅 One issue per year · inaugural issue early 2027
  • 🔓 Open to all, JALT membership not required
  • 🕶️ Double-blind peer review
  • 📐 APA 7th Edition, Word (.docx) submissions
  • 🎓 Dedicated Student Section with developmental feedback
  • 🎤 Proceedings Section for conference presenters
  • 🌐 Japanese-language abstracts warmly encouraged

Five Ways to Contribute

Academic Research Article

Original empirical or theoretical research. 3,000–5,000 words.

Practice-Based Article

Classroom experiences, lesson design, program descriptions. 2,000–4,000 words.

Student Section

For students and recent graduates, with developmental review. 1,500–3,000 words.

Proceedings Section

For Okinawa JALT conference presenters. Open 3 months after the conference.

Book Review

Critical review of a recent work in language education. 500–1,000 words.

Read the Author Guidelines → Submit Your Article

The Okinawa JALT Journal is managed by the Publications Chair with support from the editorial board. Questions about submissions? Contact us.
