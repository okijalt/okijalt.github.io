Welcome to OkiJALT!

Since 1980, Okinawa JALT has served language educators with two core missions: professional development for language teachers and promoting language education in the community. Our events feature world-renowned scholars alongside local language professionals, creating a dynamic space for sharing ideas and improving teaching quality.

  • 📚 Monthly presentations on teaching techniques and research

  • 🎉 End-of-year Bonnenkai celebrations
  • ✍️ "Power of the Pen" writing contests for high school students
  • 👥 "My Share" sessions for local teachers to exchange ideas

Constitutional Bylaws

A new bylaw setting term limitations was amended on November 27, 2020.

Term Limitations of National Officer Positions

A. Length of Term: All members elected as national officers shall serve no longer than three (3) consecutive years in one position, with the exception of Treasurer. No other member can serve as an officer for more than 6 years total.

B. Commencement of Term: The term of office for all elected national officers shall begin immediately following the election results at the Annual General Meeting.

Expanded Leadership

Okinawa JALT is growing its executive team with two new officer positions to better serve our members and community

NEW POSITION
🌊

Vice President

Executive Leadership

The Vice President supports the President in all chapter operations and serves as Acting President when needed. This role provides vital leadership continuity and ensures our chapter runs smoothly across all functions.

Key Responsibilities

  • ✦ Acts on behalf of the President in their absence
  • ✦ Oversees inter-officer communication and coordination
  • ✦ Supports planning for chapter events and initiatives
  • ✦ Liaises with JALT national and other chapters
  • ✦ Assists with strategic planning and goal-setting
NEW POSITION
📖

Publications Chair

Research & Publications

The Publications Chair leads all chapter publishing activities, including the new Okinawa JALT Journal. This role champions member scholarship, manages editorial workflows, and helps Okinawa JALT contribute meaningfully to the wider EFL research community.

Key Responsibilities

  • ✦ Oversees the Okinawa JALT Journal from submission to publication
  • ✦ Recruits and coordinates peer reviewers and editorial board
  • ✦ Manages chapter newsletter and website content
  • ✦ Promotes member publication opportunities
  • ✦ Maintains archival records of chapter publications

These positions are part of Okinawa JALT's ongoing commitment to building a sustainable, member-driven chapter. For more on our full leadership structure, see the Chapter Officers section or explore our Advisory & Leadership Boards.

Advisory & Leadership Framework

Four boards that connect experience, expertise, and community — strengthening Okinawa JALT from within and beyond

🏛️

Presidential Advisory Council

Strategic Guidance

A small council of senior members who advise the President on strategy, major decisions, and long-term direction. Provides continuity across leadership transitions and helps the chapter navigate complex challenges.

Membership: 3–5 senior/past presidents
Meetings: Quarterly or as needed
Role: Advisory (non-voting)
⚙️

Executive Officers Council

Operational Leadership

The core governing body of the chapter, comprising all elected and appointed officers. Coordinates chapter activities, allocates resources, and sets priorities for each program year — the engine of day-to-day chapter operations.

Membership: All current officers
Meetings: Monthly
Role: Advisory (non-voting)
🎓

Intercollegiate Advisory Board

Academic Partnerships

Representatives from Okinawa's universities and colleges who help align chapter programming with institutional needs. This board strengthens our academic partnerships, supports student engagement, and opens doors for collaborative research and events.

Membership: Faculty representatives from partner institutions
Meetings: Biannual
Role: Advisory & liaison
🌟

Honorary Board of Advisers

Distinguished Recognition

Distinguished scholars, educators, and community leaders invited to lend their expertise and prestige to Okinawa JALT. Honorary advisers provide mentorship, open networks, and elevate the chapter's profile in the regional and national EFL community.

Membership: By invitation only
Meetings: Annual symposium
Role: Mentorship & ambassadorship

A Framework Built for the Future

Together, these four boards create a connected leadership ecosystem — one where wisdom guides strategy, officers drive operations, universities deepen our reach, and distinguished advisers amplify our voice. This framework positions Okinawa JALT for sustainable growth and deeper community impact.

Okinawa JALT Journal

A new practitioner journal for and by Okinawa's EFL educators — bridging classroom experience and scholarly inquiry

Our Mission

The Okinawa JALT Journal is a peer-reviewed practitioner publication dedicated to the professional development of English language educators across Okinawa. It provides a venue for sharing classroom research, reflective practice, innovative pedagogy, and professional insights rooted in the Okinawa context.

The journal aims to be accessible to practitioners at all levels — from experienced researchers to teachers publishing for the first time — while maintaining high editorial standards through rigorous peer review.

Journal Highlights

  • 📄 Peer-reviewed research articles
  • 🏫 Classroom practice reports
  • 🔍 Action research narratives

Submission Categories

Research Articles

Original empirical or theoretical research on EFL teaching and learning in Okinawa or Japan contexts. 3,000–6,000 words.

Practice Reports

Descriptions and analyses of classroom activities, materials, or techniques with practical takeaways. 1,500–3,000 words.

Reflective Essays

Thoughtful narratives on teaching philosophy, professional development journeys, or significant classroom moments. 1,000–2,500 words.

Book & Resource Reviews

Critical evaluations of recent EFL publications, teaching materials, apps, or conference proceedings. 500–1,200 words.
